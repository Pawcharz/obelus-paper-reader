## What's new in 0.3.0

### Handwritten maths becomes LaTeX (Windows)

- **Found by itself.** Write a comment and a formula in the same note, e.g. *"Multi-step ELBO ∑…"*, and the words stay text while the formula becomes typeset maths (`$…$`). There is no button to press. A note that is only a formula becomes one maths block.
- **Read on your computer** by a small model (Uni-MuMER, run by llama.cpp on the processor; no graphics card needed). The first time a note needs it, an orange bar offers the download (2.7 GB). **Not now** is remembered, and the note keeps the words Windows Ink read.
- **Read twice.** Where the two readings differ, the note's menu says **Check this** and offers the other version with one tap.
- **The paper's own symbols as suggestions.** If the paper writes `\mathbb{E}` or `\Theta` where you wrote `E` or `\theta`, that spelling is offered under "The paper writes". It is never applied by itself.
- **Several lines** of a derivation are read line by line and lined up at the `=`.
- **Function names and punctuation:** `p_d(o|a)` stays one piece, and a `?` or `,` after a formula goes back to the text.

### Handwriting becomes text on Linux

Until now, handwriting stayed as ink on Linux. Now a model that runs on your computer (Qwen3.5-4B, 3.4 GB download, offered the first time you write a note) reads whole notes, maths included as `$…$`. Notes are read one at a time; the notes pane says *(reading…)* meanwhile. It needs about 4 GB of free memory and takes around ten seconds or more per note.

### Settings → Reading on this computer

- Download, cancel, resume or remove the models. Downloads continue where they stopped, and each file is checked against a fixed checksum.
- **Find maths written among the words of a note** (Windows): untick it to switch the maths reader off entirely.
- Models stop after 15 minutes without use, to give the memory back, and run at low priority so the app stays responsive.

### Sign in with Google (optional)

Click the account button at the top of the left rail. All your computers then keep their notes under the same account, so with the library on OneDrive (or any synced folder), every computer shows the same margin notes, marks, ink and side notes. Notes made on a computer before it signed in can be moved into the account. Sign-in only tells the app who you are: it gets no access to your Google data, keeps no token, and uploads nothing.

### Also new

- **HTML reports.** **Add PDF or HTML report…** (or dropping a `.html` file) turns a report into a PDF you can write on, with scroll boxes, tabs and folded parts opened out. **Original report ↗** opens the original in your browser. Images the report links from beside it are not copied.
- **The app reopens where you left it:** the same papers in the same tabs, each at the same page, position and zoom. The notes pane also reopens on the same tab and scroll position.
- **A circle round several lines** is drawn as one outline round the passage, instead of a box per line. Old marks get the new look too.
- The library folder setting moved into **Settings**.

### Good to know

- Both reading models were tuned on one person's handwriting. If they read yours badly, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Pawcharz/obelus-paper-reader/issues/new/choose).
- The models and llama.cpp are downloaded from Hugging Face and GitHub only when you choose to; their licenses are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](https://github.com/Pawcharz/obelus-paper-reader/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
